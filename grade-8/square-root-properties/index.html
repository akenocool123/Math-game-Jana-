Scene 2 · Product property

A root can split across multiplication

For non-negative numbers, the square root of a product equals the product of the square roots.

√(a × b) = √a × √b
√36 = √(4 × 9) = √4 × √9 = 2 × 3 = 6

Scene 5 · Important boundary

Roots do not split across addition

The product and quotient rules work because multiplication and division preserve the square structure. Addition does not.

√(9 + 16) = √25 = 5
√9 + √16 = 3 + 4 = 7
Therefore: √(a + b) is generally not equal to √a + √b.
